Privacy Policy

Food Label Decoder
Last updated: July 9, 2026

Food Label Decoder (“the app”) helps you understand common food package claims and run a short package checklist while you shop.

What we collect

The app does not require an account and does not collect personal information on our servers. This version does not include analytics SDKs, advertising trackers, or login systems.

What stays on your device

If you use Package check or save claims, that information is stored only on your device (for example, product names you type, checklist answers, notes, and favorites). Uninstalling the app removes that local data according to your device settings.

Internet access

Core features work without an internet connection. If you tap support email or this privacy page, your device uses its normal apps and network connection.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. Please do not send personal information of children through support email.

Changes

If the app’s practices change, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date.
