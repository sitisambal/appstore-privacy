Privacy Policy

GuideLayer (QR Product Manuals)
Last updated: July 24, 2026

GuideLayer helps Shopify merchants create mobile product guides and permanent QR codes for packaging. This policy describes how we handle data when you install and use the app.

Who we are

GuideLayer is operated by the developer account that listed the app on the Shopify App Store (InfraTech / support contact below).

Data we access through Shopify

The app requests only the Admin API scopes needed to run:

  • read_products — to list products and variants so you can link a guide to a product (optional).

We do not request customer or order scopes for core features. We do not sell personal data.

Data we store

  • Shop domain and installation status
  • Guide content you create (titles, sections, media you upload)
  • QR route identifiers and packaging destinations
  • Optional product–guide links
  • Privacy-conscious analytics (guide opens, searches, helpful yes/no) without full IP addresses or advertising trackers
  • Session tokens required for Shopify embedded authentication

Public guide pages

When a guide is live, customers can open it via a link or QR code without logging in. Feedback buttons store only yes/no style signals for the merchant, not the customer’s name or email (unless a future feature you enable collects more, which would update this policy).

Photos and files

Photos you upload for guides are stored so they can be shown in the guide. Use photos you have rights to use. Do not upload personal data of others without a lawful reason.

Billing

Paid plans are charged through Shopify Billing. Shopify processes payment details according to Shopify’s policies.

Retention and uninstall

If you uninstall the app, we stop billable processing. Public guides may remain reachable for a short recovery window so printed QR codes do not break immediately, then shop data is deleted when Shopify’s shop redaction requirements apply or our retention period ends.

Security

We use HTTPS, Shopify session tokens for admin access, and minimize scopes. No system is perfect; contact us if you suspect a problem.

Children

The app is for merchants, not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If practices change, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date.
