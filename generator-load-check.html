Privacy Policy

Generator Load Check
Last updated: August 21, 2026

Generator Load Check helps you plan what a portable generator can run. It is a planning aid, not an electrician.

What we collect

The app does not require an account and does not collect personal information on our servers. This version has no analytics SDKs, advertising trackers, or login.

What stays on your device

Generator size, selected appliances, custom items, and kits stay on this phone. Uninstalling removes that data according to your device settings.

Internet

Core features work offline. If you open support email or this page, your device uses the network as usual.
