Privacy Policy

Generator Wattage Calculator (formerly Generator Load Check)
Last updated: September 24, 2026

Generator Wattage Calculator helps you plan what a portable generator can run. It is a planning aid, not an electrician.

What we collect

The app does not require an account and does not collect personal information on our servers. This version has no analytics SDKs, advertising trackers, or login.

What stays on your device

Generator size, selected appliances, custom items, and kits stay on this phone. Uninstalling removes that data according to your device settings.

Purchases

The optional one-time Full plan unlock is sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, payment details or Apple ID. The app asks the App Store whether this Apple ID owns the unlock, and remembers the answer on this phone so it works offline.

Internet

Core features work offline. The App Store is contacted only to show the price, complete a purchase or restore one. If you open support email or this page, your device uses the network as usual.
